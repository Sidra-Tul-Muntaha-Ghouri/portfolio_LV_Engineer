FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to send for a quote?

Any of: single line diagram, load list, specification, preferred enclosure system and deadline. If you only have a rough idea, send that and we will ask the right questions.

How quickly can you deliver?

It depends on scope. Every quote comes with a firm delivery date, and urgent jobs can often be prioritised.

Which software and standards do you use?

EPLAN Electric P8, EPLAN ProPanel, AutoCAD, ETAP, Simaris, Easy Prisma, PVsyst and Helioscope, with designs following IEC standards.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. You receive PDFs plus the native source files so your team can modify and reuse them.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. We regularly collaborate with international clients and teams remotely across time zones.
