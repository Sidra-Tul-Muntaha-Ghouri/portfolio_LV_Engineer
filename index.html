Power system studies

Confirm the system is right before it is energised

Our studies are carried out in ETAP and delivered as a clear report that your client, consultant or authority can review.

A

Load Flow

Verify voltage profiles, transformer and cable loading, and power factor under present and future scenarios.

B

Short Circuit

Calculate fault levels to confirm that breaker and busbar ratings are adequate across the network.

C

Protection Coordination

Time-current coordination so only the device nearest the fault trips and the rest of the plant stays running.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to send for a quote?

Send any of the following: a single line diagram, load list, specification, preferred enclosure system and your deadline. If you only have a rough idea, send that and we will ask the right questions.

How do dedicated engineers work?

We assign experienced design engineers to your projects. They follow your standards and templates and work to your deadlines, either per project or on an ongoing basis.

How quickly can you deliver?

It depends on the scope. Every quote includes a firm delivery date, and urgent jobs can often be prioritised.

Which software and standards do you use?

We work in EPLAN Electric P8, EPLAN ProPanel, AutoCAD, ETAP, Simaris and Easy Prisma, and our designs follow IEC standards.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. You receive PDFs and the native source files so your team can modify and reuse them.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work remotely with international clients and teams across time zones.
