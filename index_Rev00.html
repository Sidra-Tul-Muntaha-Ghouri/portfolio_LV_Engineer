FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to send for a quote?

Any of: single line diagram, load list, project specification, enclosure system preference and your deadline. If you only have a rough idea, send that and I will ask the right questions.

How quickly can you deliver?

It depends on scope. Most panel designs and studies are quoted with a firm delivery date, and urgent jobs can often be prioritised.

Which software and standards do you work with?

EPLAN Electric P8, EPLAN ProPanel, AutoCAD, ETAP, Simaris, Easy Prisma, PVsyst and Helioscope, with designs following IEC standards.

Will I get editable files?

Yes. You receive PDFs plus the native source files so your team can modify and reuse them.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I regularly collaborate with international clients and teams remotely across time zones.
